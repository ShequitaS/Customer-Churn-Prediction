SECTION 4 — COST-BENEFIT: WHERE DO WE SET THE THRESHOLD?
======================================================================
Assumptions (change these and the answer changes — that is the point):
  Retention offer cost      : $100 per customer contacted
  Customer lifetime value   : $1500
  Offer success rate        : 30% of true churners contacted are saved

Economics per contacted customer:
  True churner  -> spend $100, save them 30% of the time
                   => expected value = 0.3 x $1500 - $100 = $350
  Non-churner   -> spend $100 on someone who was never leaving
                   => expected value = -$100

   Contact 728 of 2,500 customers (29% of the book)
   Catch 386 of 509 churners (recall 76%)
   Precision 53% — 342 contacts wasted on customers who would have stayed
   NET VALUE: $100,900
